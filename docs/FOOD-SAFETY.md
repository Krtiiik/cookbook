# Food-safety sources

Source order for every food-safety number on the site: first a Czech or EU authority (SZÚ, bezpecnostpotravin.cz (the Ministerstvo zemědělství portal), SZPI, Ministerstvo zdravotnictví and its regional public health offices (KHS), EFSA, EU regulations). Next, another European authority (BfR in Germany, FSA/NHS in the UK). USDA FSIS, FoodKeeper or NCHFP only when no Czech or European source gives a number. When sources differ, the site uses the stricter number. In the Czech text, name the source short: "podle SZÚ", "podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz", "podle KHS", "podle BfR", "podle britského úřadu FSA", "podle USDA FSIS". Numbers below were read from the pages on 2026-11-24.

Note: decree 137/2004 Sb. used to set 75 °C for 5 minutes for cooking, 70 °C for reheating and 5 days for chilled dishes (§§ 21, 31, 32). These sections are repealed since 1 Jan 2007 (current text: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-137). Do not cite them. The copy on bezpecnostpotravin.cz is the old 2004 text.

## 1. Cooked leftovers (guláš, vývar, kaše, karamelizovaná cibule, vařené brambory)

| Item | Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooling | Shallow containers, into the fridge within 2 hours | podle KHS / podle SZÚ | KHS Středočeského kraje, "Pravidla pro zacházení s potravinami a pokrmy" (2013): chill or freeze within 2 hours, use shallow containers with lids, keep at ≤4 °C. https://khsstc.cz/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Pravidla_pro_zachazeni_s_potravinami_a_pokrmy.pdf. SZÚ: do not leave cooked food at room temperature for more than 2 hours. https://szu.gov.cz/aktuality/proc-byva-v-lete-vice-pripadu-alimentarnich-onemocneni-a-jak-na-prevenci/ |
| Fridge | **2 days** (absolute maximum 3) | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz | Glossary "Lednička": throw away leftovers that are in the fridge "déle než dva, nejvýše tři dny". https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/termin/lednicka/ |
| Freezer (quality) | About 2–3 months | podle britského úřadu FSA | FSA: frozen meat is best within 2–3 months; eat leftovers within 48 hours or freeze them. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/how-to-chill-freeze-and-defrost-food-safely/how-to-chill-freeze-and-defrost-food-safely |

Disagreement: Fórum zdravé výživy on bezpecnostpotravin.cz says 1–2 days (https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/jak-zachazet-s-potravinami/), FSA 48 hours, BfR 2–3 days (https://www.bfr.bund.de/fragen-und-antworten/thema/vorkochen-fuer-mehrere-tage/), USDA 3–4 days. One article on bezpecnostpotravin.cz (Společnost pro výživu, 2019) says up to 5 days; it follows the repealed catering rule. Stricter: 2 days. No Czech or EU number for frozen cooked dishes; BfR gives 3–6 months for frozen beef and pork, so FSA's 2–3 months is the stricter European number.

## 2. Reheating leftovers

| Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Heat until it is hot all the way through and simmers; in the core **at least 75 °C, or 70 °C for 2 minutes** | podle SZÚ a BfR | SZÚ, "Časté alimentární nákazy…" (2022): 75 °C, or 70 °C for 2 min (or 67 °C 5 min, 64 °C 13 min) in the whole volume; reheated food at least 70 °C in the core. https://szu.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/alim_nakazy_shrnuti_FIN_2022.pdf. BfR (2025): reheat to at least 70 °C for 2 minutes at every point. https://www.bfr.bund.de/fragen-und-antworten/thema/vorkochen-fuer-mehrere-tage/ |

Disagreement: SZÚ minimum for reheating is 70 °C; bezpecnostpotravin.cz glossary "Nebezpečný interval teplot" says 74 °C (https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/termin/nebezpecny-interval-teplot/); a Společnost pro výživu article on bezpecnostpotravin.cz says at least 75 °C within 1 hour of taking food from the fridge (https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/jak-je-to-s-ohrivanim-doma-varenych-pokrmu/); FSA says "steaming hot throughout" and reheat only once. Stricter: 75 °C (or 70 °C held 2 min), which replaces USDA 74 °C.

## 3. Raw beef (whole cut, cubes)

| Item | Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fridge | **Cook within 2 days**, whole or cubed | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz | Prof. Dostálová (Fórum zdravé výživy, 2020): do not keep shop meat long in the fridge, cook it "do dvou dnů". https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/jak-zachazet-s-potravinami/ |
| Freezer, safety | Safe at −18 °C almost without limit | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz | Same article: at −18 °C and below microbial growth stops, so frozen meat keeps "téměř neomezeně"; cook it soon after thawing. |
| Freezer, quality | 3–6 months | podle BfR | BfR: beef and pork 3–6 months at −18 °C. https://www.bfr.bund.de/fragen-und-antworten/thema/vorkochen-fuer-mehrere-tage/ |

Disagreement: USDA gives 3–5 days for whole cuts and 1–2 days for cubes. bezpecnostpotravin.cz freezer table gives beef 6–12 months, lean beef 12 months and more; FSA gives meat 2–3 months. Stricter safety number: 2 days for all raw beef. For quality, BfR's 3–6 months is the stricter of the Czech and German numbers. EU legal storage temperature (business rule, Regulation 853/2004 Annex III, Section I, Chapter VII): meat ≤7 °C, offal ≤3 °C, minced meat ≤2 °C. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/853/annex/III/adopted

## 4. Variety meats (líčka, oháňka, vnitřnosti)

| Item | Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fridge | 1–2 days | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz a USDA FSIS | Czech: meat within 2 days (topic 3). The day range 1–2 for variety meats is USDA only. |
| Freezer, quality | 3–4 months | podle USDA FSIS | bezpecnostpotravin.cz table: "Vepřové maso, vnitřnosti" 3–6 months, pork liver 3 months or less. USDA 3–4 months is stricter, so it stays. |

Regulation 853/2004 keeps offal at ≤3 °C against ≤7 °C for other meat, which supports the shorter fridge time. No Czech/EU source classes cheeks or oxtail as offal; that grouping stays "podle USDA FSIS".

## 5. Raw beef bones

Freeze on the day you buy them, or keep 1–2 days in the fridge (as topic 4). Safe at −18 °C almost without limit, "podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz" (https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/jak-zachazet-s-potravinami/). No source has a number for bones; the quality limit 3–4 months stays "podle USDA FSIS".

## 6. Butter

**No Czech/EU home number. USDA FoodKeeper stays** (fridge 1–2 months, freezer 6–9 months, quality). Czech law has only labelling rules: "čerstvé máslo" within 20 days of production; "stolní máslo" stored at ≤−18 °C for at most 24 months (vyhláška 397/2016 Sb., per bezpecnostpotravin.cz, https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/termin/oznacovani-tuku/). These are not home storage advice; do not use them as such.

## 7. Garlic in oil

| Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Better not to make garlic in oil for storage. If you make it: fridge at once and **eat it at the latest the day after you make it**, or freeze it. Never at room temperature. | podle BfR (bezpecnostpotravin.cz) | BfR, botulism Q&A (12 Nov 2025): keep in the fridge, eat "spätestens am Tag nach der Herstellung"; BfR advises against home stocks. https://www.bfr.bund.de/de/fragen_und_antworten_zu_botulismus-70355.html (Czech translation on the MZe portal, 25 Nov 2025: https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/bfr-otazky-a-odpovedi-o-botulismu/) |

Disagreement: NCHFP gives ≤4 °C and 4 days. BfR is stricter: 1 day. Ministerstvo zdravotnictví names garlic in oil as a typical source of botulinum toxin (2026 pâté warning, https://mzd.gov.cz/varovani-pred-konzumaci-potraviny-pastika-s-nazvem-hotel-litovel-srdce-luhacovic/). The ≤4 °C fridge number can stay (KHS, topic 10).
## 8. Opened tomato paste

| Item | Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| From a tin | Move it to a non-metal container with a lid and **use within 2 days** | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz | Glossary "Lednička": do not keep food in opened tins; move it to non-metal containers, close, "spotřebujte do 2 dnů". https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/termin/lednicka/ |
| Mould | Throw away the whole jar | podle BfR | BfR mould FAQ (11 Mar 2026): mouldy food must not be eaten; mould can grow deep inside where you cannot see it; only dry-cured salami and whole hams are exceptions. https://www.bfr.bund.de/fragen-und-antworten/thema/schimmelpilze-in-lebensmitteln-gesundheitliche-risiken-und-wie-sie-sich-vermeiden-lassen/ |

Disagreement: USDA 5–7 days. The Czech rule is for opened tins in general, not tomato paste only, but it is the only Czech number and it is stricter. For tubes and jars, the label ("po otevření spotřebujte do…") stays. The USDA reason "mould lowers acidity" can stay only as "podle USDA FSIS"; BfR's reason is mycotoxins and hidden growth.

## 9. Core temperatures for meat (thermometer page)

| Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|
| All meat, poultry, minced meat and leftovers: core **75 °C, or 70 °C for 2 minutes** | podle SZÚ | SZÚ 2022 PDF (topic 2). |
| Same rule for meat, poultry and minced meat | podle BfR | BfR salmonella FAQ (27 Sep 2024): meat must reach at least 70 °C for 2 minutes in the core. https://www.bfr.bund.de/fragen-und-antworten/thema/fragen-und-antworten-zum-schutz-vor-infektionen-mit-salmonellen/ |
| Whole cuts of beef and lamb (steak, joint) may stay pink inside after you sear the outside; not poultry, pork, minced, rolled or skewered meat, burgers, offal | podle britského úřadu FSA | FSA "Cooking your food": core 70 °C for 2 min (also 60 °C 45 min, 65 °C 10 min, 75 °C 30 s, 80 °C 6 s); exceptions as listed. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cooking-your-food/cooking-your-food |
| Colour of meat or juice is not a reliable sign | podle USDA FSIS | **No Czech/EU source says this directly; USDA stays.** KHS calls the thermometer the most exact method and the visual check only common practice; FSA gives colour and clear juices only as a fallback without a thermometer. |

Disagreement: USDA 63 °C + 3 min rest (whole beef/pork/veal/lamb), 71 °C (minced), 74 °C (poultry, leftovers) are all lower than SZÚ's 75 °C or 70 °C/2 min. Stricter: the SZÚ numbers for every row. For whole beef and lamb cuts the FSA pink exception is a doneness choice, not a safe-core number; say so if the page keeps it. The KHS leaflet says 75 °C in the whole volume, which matches SZÚ.

| Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fridge at most 5 °C; perishable food in the coldest part at 0–4 °C. Freezer −18 °C. | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz | Glossary "Lednička": "nejvýše 5 °C", coldest part 0–4 °C, freezer −18 °C. https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/termin/lednicka/. ## 11. Potatoes in the fridge and acrylamide

| Use on the site | Cite | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Store raw potatoes dark, cool and dry, above 8 °C for less acrylamide; not in the fridge | podle bezpecnostpotravin.cz | Glossary "Uchovávání ovoce, zeleniny a brambor": 4–7 °C is best against sprouting, but for acrylamide "vhodnější teplota nad 8 °C", with fewer reducing sugars; keep away from light so the tubers do not go green. https://bezpecnostpotravin.cz/termin/uchovavani-ovoce-zeleniny-a-brambor/ |
| EU rule for food businesses: store potatoes above 6 °C | podle nařízení EU 2017/2158 | Annex I, potato storage: "above 6 °C". https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/2158/annex/I/adopted (EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/2158/oj) |

Disagreement: FSA (UK) changed its advice: the fridge is acceptable at home, as a study reviewed by COT found no material rise in acrylamide potential (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/acrylamide/acrylamide). FDA (US) still advises against the fridge. Stricter: not in the fridge; the Czech and EU sources now support this, so the page can cite them in place of FDA. EFSA (2015) is the basis for the acrylamide cancer risk; it gives no storage number.
